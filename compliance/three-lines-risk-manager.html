Risk Manager
"I think we were within mandate" is not oversight. Real oversight means being able to prove it - to your board, your auditor, regulator, and clients, at any point in time, without violating rules and looking unprofessional.
What is yields.digital?

yields.digital's LYRA (Layered Yields & Risks Assessment) gives risk managers continuous, on-demand risk assessment of digital asset positions - with a timestamped evidence trail that accumulates with each assessment and is exportable on demand (takes seconds). Not a dashboard you have to remember to check. A system that fires when something needs your attention and documents everything, whether you were watching or not.

LYRA was designed and is constantly being improved by a team of very senior ex-financial services professionals, which includes a CFA with over 8 years of institutional quantitative portfolio management.

Apply consistent methodology across all protocols and asset types
Live
The Challenge Without LYRA
Different protocols demand different analytical frameworks. Cross-portfolio comparisons are inconsistent, difficult to defend to a board, and impossible to automate at scale.
What LYRA Gives You
The identical four position-level dimension (Asset, Pool, Protocol, Chain) methodology applied uniformly to every protocol, rolled up into a holistic portfolio risk score. Where nuances between certain assets or protocols appear, we work in a structured quantitative approach to normalise and ensure clients receive consistent data. Fully comparable, fully auditable.
Monitor a growing number of protocols and positions simultaneously
Q2 2026
The Challenge Without LYRA
One analyst cannot monitor ten or more protocols continuously without missing events. Human monitoring does not scale as the investment universe grows - and the gaps are where risk lives.
